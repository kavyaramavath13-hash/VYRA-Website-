01 / ABOUT VYRA

Technology should feel like a helping hand.

VYRA is a student-led venture focused on exploring practical technology that makes everyday life a little simpler.

We’re curious about how AI and thoughtful design can take friction out of ordinary tasks. Our starting point is simple: useful technology should be easy to understand, accessible to more people, and built around real everyday needs.
